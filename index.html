About The Engineer

Welcome to MK Corporation. I am an experienced electrical engineer specializing in advanced diagnostics and precise component-level repair of electronic devices and electrical systems.

With a deep understanding of circuit design, micro-soldering, and troubleshooting protocols, I restore functionality to devices that others might consider beyond repair. My approach is methodical, ensuring that not only is the symptom addressed, but the root cause is resolved.
